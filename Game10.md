When you take part in such battles, the emotions and excitement stay in your memory for the rest of your life.  
Of course, on closer consideration, one can find mistakes and inaccuracies on both sides, but without them there would be no interesting games.  
Live people are playing, with their own emotions, and sometimes they simply don’t even have the energy to calculate all the possibilities.  
It’s easy to offer authoritative advice in analysis after spending a long time looking at a position.  
Analysis will show which side had the advantage and who was closer to the truth.  
But we must remember that during a game, two minds and two characters are competing with one another, each with a hundred pros and cons.  
And when both are hanging over the abyss, when the outcome of the battle is decided by a single tempo or turn of the move, there is no place for mathematical truth.  
You simply have to win and stay on your feet, clinging to the stones with your hands to keep your balance.  
However, perhaps these are just my fantasies, which regularly arise in my mind when I sit down at the board and the initial position of Dameo is in front of me...
